# Validation Checklist

Mark each item pass or revise, with one line of reason for any revise.

## Contents
- Per key result
- Per OKR

## Per key result
- **Who**: a specific human audience, named, not "users" or "the business".
- **Does what**: a behavior change observable by a third party.
- **By how much**: a number or ratio, with a baseline or an explicit "baseline unknown".
- **Outcome**: contains no task, deliverable, feature, or system metric.
- **Controllable**: the team's own levers can plausibly move it.
- **Solution-free**: allows several different ideas to be tried.

## Per OKR
- **One objective**, two to three key results.
- **Proof**: reaching all key results would convincingly show the objective was achieved.
- **Alignment**: traces to a parent strategy or objective.
- **Team-level**: not assigned to an individual or tied to compensation.
